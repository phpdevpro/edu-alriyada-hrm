HRM and Education Module Integration

1. Overview

The HRM module and the Education module are connected so that faculty members can be managed both as employees and as academic instructors. HRM manages the employment side, while Education manages the teaching and academic side.

2. How the Connection Works

HRM Module Education Module Integration Purpose
Employee Instructor A faculty member can exist as both an employee and an instructor.
Employee department Instructor department The Education module can use the employee's department data.
Employee gender and identity details Instructor profile Basic HR information is available for academic records and reporting.
Jameah demographic and compliance fields Instructor profile Faculty records can support ministry and academic reporting requirements.
Institute placement and facility assignment Instructor profile Education can identify where the faculty member belongs academically and physically.

3. Functional Responsibility

Area Owned By Description
Employment record HRM Joining date, employment status, employee record, and HR master information.
Teaching profile Education Instructor usage in scheduling, course assignment, attendance, and academic workflows.
Faculty identity consistency Shared Both modules should use the same linked employee and instructor data.
Current Status: The Education module already contains a direct Instructor-to-Employee relationship. This confirms that HRM and Education are already structurally connected.

4. Client Summary

In simple terms, HRM manages the person as an employee, and Education manages the same person as an instructor. This avoids duplicate records and keeps faculty information aligned across administrative and academic operations.
