# HRM Operations SOP

## Daily Steps

1. Review newly submitted requests.
2. Validate employee eligibility and required attachments.
3. Approve or reject with clear comments.
4. Route financial-impact requests to Accounts Manager.
5. Confirm terminal statuses (`Approved`, `Rejected`, `Issued`, `Completed`).

## Weekly Steps

1. Audit pending requests by age.
2. Escalate stuck requests to approvers.
3. Validate status consistency and close completed requests.

## Monthly Steps

1. Export HR request metrics (volume, approvals, rejections, SLA).
2. Reconcile overtime/training/allowance approvals with payroll inputs.
3. Validate medical license expiry list and renewals.

## Controls

- Do not bypass HR gate for any employee-origin request.
- Finance-impact requests must include Accounts Manager review.
- All approvals/rejections must include remarks.
