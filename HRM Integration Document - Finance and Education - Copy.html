HRM Connection with Education and Finance

1. Purpose

This document provides a short summary of how the HRM module connects with the Education module and how it is planned to connect with the Finance module.

2. HRM Connection with Education Module

The Education module is already available in the system. Based on the current implementation, the HRM module connects with Education through the relationship between Employee and Instructor.

HRM Side Education Side Connection
Employee Instructor The Instructor record contains a direct link to Employee.
Employee Department Instructor Department Instructor department is fetched from the linked Employee record.
Employee Gender Instructor Gender Instructor gender is fetched from the linked Employee record.
HR faculty/staff identity Academic teaching identity HR manages employment data, while Education uses Instructor for teaching operations.
Confirmed from current Education module: The Education app already uses Instructor in academic workflows such as class scheduling, course assignment, attendance-related structures, and instructor-based academic records.

3. HRM Connection with Finance Module

The Finance module connection is not yet fully developed. However, the HRM module will become the source of employee and payroll-related data for Finance.

HRM Data Finance Usage Status
Employee master data Payroll eligibility and employee financial records Planned
Employment status Control whether salary should be processed Planned
Department and designation Salary grouping, reporting, and approval structure Planned
Attendance, leave, and overtime Payroll calculation inputs Planned
Allowances and deductions Salary slip and accounting integration Planned

4. Finance Integration Roadmap

Phase Planned Work
Phase 1 Connect Employee master data with payroll-related finance records.
Phase 2 Use attendance, leave, and overtime from HRM as payroll inputs.
Phase 3 Map salary structures, allowances, and deductions to Finance processing.
Phase 4 Generate salary slips, payroll entries, and accounting postings.
Phase 5 Build reporting by employee, department, faculty, and cost center.

5. Summary

The HRM module is already connected with the Education module through the Employee and Instructor relationship. HRM provides the employment identity, while Education uses Instructor for academic operations.

The Finance connection is planned next. HRM will provide the employee, status, attendance, leave, overtime, and compensation-related data required for payroll and financial processing.
